WHAT A GOOD REFERRAL CONTAINS

Reason for referral in one sentence
Which eye(s), and the findings that matter
Urgency and why
Image quality, so the receiver knows how far to trust the photograph
Who approved the decision — and that a human did
Nothing identifying in a free-text field that is not needed

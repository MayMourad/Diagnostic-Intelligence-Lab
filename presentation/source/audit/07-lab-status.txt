WHAT IS IMPLEMENTED

Confusion matrix, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, accuracy, AUC and Wilson 95 % intervals — unit-tested against hand-checked tables.
Held-out import with patient-level leakage, duplicate and split checks that block reporting.
Threshold selection on validation data only, applied once to the test partition.

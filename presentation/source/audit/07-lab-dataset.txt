LIMITATIONS A REAL MODEL WOULD CARRY

Domain shift: a model trained on one camera or clinic may fail on another.
Population shift: pigmentation, age and co-morbidity differ between datasets and sites.
Prevalence shift: PPV changes with the screened population even if sensitivity does not.
Reference-label noise: expert graders disagree; the “truth” is itself uncertain.
Ungradable images: a model must be able to return “no result”; forcing a grade is unsafe.
Automation bias: clinicians may over-trust a displayed score. This must be studied, not assumed away.
Calibration: a score of 0.8 does not mean 80 % unless calibration is checked.
No evidence from this project that AI improves clinician diagnostic accuracy.

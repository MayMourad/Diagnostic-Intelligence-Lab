UNCERTAINTY REGISTER

What limits confidence in this one case.

One image of one eye, with no clinical history, no second field, no OCT. Real screening uses more.
This is a synthetic teaching illustration. Real fundus photographs vary in ways that are not represented here (camera, pigmentation, media opacity, co-existing disease).
The reference label is one authored standard. Inter-grader variability — a real source of uncertainty — is not quantified for this teaching set.
